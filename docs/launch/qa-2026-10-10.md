# Pre-launch QA: https://stickerz.dev (2026-10-10)

Method: playwright-core 1.60 (gstack install) driving the cached headless Chromium 1243, against the live site. Every page was loaded at 1280×800 and 390×844, light and dark (dark set through mode-watcher's `mode-watcher-mode` key; the header theme toggle was also clicked once and works). Console errors, page errors, failed requests and 4xx/5xx responses were logged on every page, and all screenshots were reviewed. Code fixes were checked against a local `vite preview` build with the `/api/stacks` calls mocked, so nothing extra was written to the real database.

## Bugs found

| Sev | Page | Steps | What I saw | Status |
|-----|------|-------|------------|--------|
| High | /editor | Save a design, reload `/editor`, then Share (or Save again) | `savedId` is only in memory, while the layout draft lives on in localStorage. After a reload the switcher showed "QA-DELETE-002556 · Not saved yet" plus the saved copy. Share sent `POST /api/stacks` (201) and made a second row. Opening a design from My designs (`?id=`) and reloading does the same. | **Fixed** in 9d1565b1: the id is kept in `ss:saved-id` and restored unless `?id=` or `#l=` is loading something else, and a 404 on autosave clears it. Checked locally with mocked API: after reload, autosave PUTs to the same id and Share makes no new POST. |
| High | /designs/[id] | Save a design (default visibility Private) and open its `/designs/<id>` URL in a logged-out browser | Private designs return 200 and render for anyone (`getStack` ignores visibility). The Visibility control labels Private as "Only you", and `/legal/privacy` says designs stay yours. | Not fixed: needs a product decision (404 for non-owners vs. relabel Private), and the Share flow depends on it |
| Medium | /community | Click Trending → Newest → Most remixed | The order is identical every time. `listGallery` uses `remixCount desc` for both Trending and Most remixed, and with the current seed data Newest happens to give the same order too. | Not fixed: Trending needs a real ranking (e.g. remixes weighted by age) |
| Medium | /, /stickers, /community, /designs/[id] | Click "Open in the editor", "Open in editor" or "Remix" | Any `#l=` load immediately runs `POST /api/stacks` and creates a private row for that visitor, even if they never save.
