1. Using Stack Stickers

Stack Stickers is a free tool for designing laptop sticker layouts. You don’t need an account. By using the site you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use it.

2. Your lids

Layouts you make are yours. When you share one, it gets a public link and can appear in the Gallery if you post it there. You let us show, link to and make preview images of shared lids. You can delete a shared lid at any time from the link you were given.

3. Logos and trademarks

The logos come from svgl and belong to their owners. They’re here so you can make stickers for your own laptop. Don’t use Stack Stickers to make stickers or merchandise to sell unless you have permission from each brand. See the Trademark notice.

4. What not to do

Don’t upload artwork you don’t have the right to use, share lids meant to harass or mislead, or try to break or overload the site. We may remove shared lids that do.

5. Sponsorships

Brands can sponsor placements. Sponsored placements are always labeled and never change search results or what you can put on your lid.

6. No warranty

The site is provided as is. We work hard to get sizes right, but always print a test sheet before ordering. We aren’t responsible for print costs or results.

7. Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we’ll say so on this page and in the changelog.
