DOCS / PRINTING · How to print your laptop stickers · The editor exports your lid at true size, so what you see is what gets cut. Pick the route that matches what you have: a print shop, a Cricut, or a Silhouette. · 6 MIN READ · UPDATED OCT 2026

1. Export from the editor

Open your lid, press Export, and choose the file that fits how you’ll print. All three use the same layout and the same sticker sizes.

2. Check the size

Printers love to “fit to page”, which quietly shrinks everything. Always print at 100% scale. Every export has a 50 mm ruler in the corner so you can check before you cut.

TEST FIRST — [50 mm ruler graphic: 0 … 50 MM] — Print one sheet on plain paper and measure the ruler. If it isn’t 50 mm, check your print dialog for scaling before you use vinyl.

3. Print and cut

[Print shop] [Cricut] [Silhouette]
  1. Upload the PDF print sheet. It’s already set up for kiss cutting, so most shops can print it as is.
  2. Pick vinyl, matte or gloss. Ask for a laminate if the laptop travels a lot. It stops scuffs.
  3. Ask for “kiss cut”, not “die cut”. Kiss cut keeps every sticker on one backing sheet, in your layout.
  4. Order one sheet first. Check colors and edges before printing for the whole team.
  1. Upload the SVG cut file in Design Space. Use the SVG, not the PNG, so the cut path comes with it.
  2. Set Operation to Print Then Cut, then Flatten each sticker with its outline.
  3. Print on printable vinyl. Keep the layout inside Cricut’s Print Then Cut area, about 6.75 × 9.25 in. Big lids may need two sheets.
  4. Choose Printable Vinyl as the material and cut. Peel slowly from a corner.
  1. Open the PNG in Silhouette Studio. The free edition can’t open SVGs; the PNG works everywhere.
  2. Trace the outer edge with a small offset to get the white border around each logo.
  3. Turn on registration marks and print on sticker paper or printable vinyl.
  4. Load the sheet and cut with a kiss-cut setting for your material, so the backing stays whole.

4. Stick it on

Wipe the lid with a little isopropyl alcohol and let it dry. Start from the center sticker and work outward, using the placement guide that prints on the last page of the PDF. Press each one from the middle to the edges to push out air.
DOCS / GET STARTED · How it works · Three steps, no account. Your lid is drawn at real size, so the layout you make is the one you’ll print.

1. Pick your logos

Search the library of developer logos from svgl, or start from someone’s stack in the Gallery. You can also drop in your own SVG.

2. Arrange the lid

Choose your laptop and lid color, then drag, resize and rotate. Shuffle gives you a fresh layout if you’re stuck.

3. Share or print

Share a link, post it to the Gallery, or export print files. See How to print for the details.

Where your stacks live

Stacks are saved in your browser. Clearing site data deletes them, so share a link to anything you want to keep. Shared links are public to anyone who has them.

Opening a pull request

Keep PRs small and say what you tested. For anything bigger than a fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach before you spend the time.

Logos aren’t here

We don’t keep logos in this repo. They come from svgl. See Adding a missing logo.

What svgl needs

  1. An SVG from the brand’s official press kit or site.
  2. Light and dark versions, if the logo has them.
  3. A category and a link to the product.

DOCS / HELP · FAQ
Is it free?
Yes. No account, no watermark, no paid tier. It’s open source and kept running by sponsors.
Do you sell stickers?
No. You design the lid and print it yourself or through a print shop. We never sell branded merchandise.
Can I sell stickers I make here?
Not with other companies’ logos. They’re trademarks. Personal use is the idea; selling needs permission from each brand.
Which laptops are supported?
MacBook Air 13″ and 15″, and MacBook Pro 14″ and 16″. Other laptops are on the roadmap; vote for yours on GitHub.
Where are my stacks saved?
In your browser. Share a link to keep a copy that survives clearing your history.
My company’s logo is here. Can you remove it?
Yes, usually within 48 hours. Use the brand request form. No lawyer needed.
